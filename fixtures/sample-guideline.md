# Community Green Innovation Fund

## Funding guidelines

The Community Green Innovation Fund supports community-led projects that reduce waste, improve local environmental health, and make neighborhoods more resilient. Applicants should read all requirements before submitting. This guide describes application requirements and is not a determination of legal status or funding eligibility.

### Eligibility and project requirements

1. **Mandatory — Applicant status:** The applicant must be a registered non-profit organization.
2. **Mandatory — Operating history:** The applicant shall have been registered as a non-profit for at least three years on the application deadline.
3. **Mandatory — Service area:** The proposed project must primarily benefit residents of the fund's service area.
4. **Mandatory — Project purpose:** The project must demonstrate a clear environmental benefit, such as reducing waste, conserving resources, or improving local ecosystems.
5. **Mandatory — Budget cap:** The requested grant amount shall not exceed **$50,000**.
6. **Mandatory — Budget breakdown:** Applicants must provide an itemized budget breakdown showing costs, quantities, and requested grant amounts.
7. **Mandatory — Registration certificate:** A copy of the organization's current registration certificate must be included.
8. **Mandatory — Audited accounts:** The applicant must provide audited accounts for its most recently completed financial year.
9. **Mandatory — Project plan:** The application must describe activities, a delivery timeline, and who is responsible for implementation.
10. **Recommended — Sustainability plan:** Applicants should explain how project benefits will continue after grant funding ends.
11. **Recommended — Letters of support:** Letters of support from community partners are encouraged.
12. **Recommended — Outcome measures:** Applicants are encouraged to identify measurable outcomes and explain how progress will be tracked.

### Submission

Submit a complete application and the required attachments through the fund's designated application process by the published deadline. Applicants are responsible for ensuring submitted information is accurate. The fund may request clarification where information is incomplete or unclear.
